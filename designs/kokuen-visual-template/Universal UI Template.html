08

Boundary law

A component does not get a box merely because it exists. Every edge names a job and one control has one focus geometry.

  1. Name the job: input affordance, state, containment, clipping, or separation.
  2. Remove the edge when spacing, alignment, or tone already does the job.
  3. Keep fields bounded to expected content unless the task is search, command, composition, or full-row entry.
  4. Keep Clear, Edit, validation, and other field actions inside or immediately beside the field.
  5. Focus must remain visible, unclipped, high-contrast, and attached to the actual target.
  6. Never nest same-level shells to simulate depth or importance.
